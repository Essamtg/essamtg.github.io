OUR PURPOSE

About Global Trade Insights

Global Trade Insights is an independent educational project created to make international trade and commodity markets easier to understand.

We publish practical explainers about agricultural commodities, coffee, tea, sesame, gum arabic, shipping, logistics, export processes and African trade corridors.

Our editorial approach

Our goal is to explain concepts clearly, distinguish general education from time-sensitive market data, and encourage readers to verify commercial, regulatory and shipping information with the relevant authorities and counterparties before making business decisions.

Who we serve

Students, entrepreneurs, exporters, importers and anyone interested in how goods move between producers and markets.

Global Trade Insights is an educational publication. It is not a broker, freight forwarder, customs agent or financial adviser.
