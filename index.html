A practical guide to exporting coffee from East Africa

Coffee export depends on consistent quality, clear contracts, compliant documentation and careful logistics planning.

Quality and specifications

Identify the species and commercial grade. Buyers may specify screen size, defects, moisture, cup profile, processing method, crop year and packaging. Agree on a written specification and representative sample before purchase.

Documents and logistics

Common documents may include a sales contract, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and transport documents. Agricultural products may require additional certificates. Requirements depend on origin, destination and current rules.

Protect the transaction

Check counterparties, agree payment terms, define delivery responsibilities and obtain written freight quotations that identify included and excluded charges.

Sesame quality: what buyers and exporters should understand

Sesame quality is assessed through several factors. Buyer specifications should be agreed in writing before a deal is finalized.

Purity and moisture

Buyers commonly examine stones, dust, stems, other seeds and foreign material. Moisture affects storage stability; acceptable limits vary by buyer, standard and shipping conditions. Use a suitable testing method and agree on sampling.

Packaging and inspection

Confirm bag type, weight, labels, lot identification and any liner requirements. Specify who samples and inspects the goods, which standards apply and how claims are resolved.

Export documents: a beginner's checklist

Export documents identify goods, record the transaction, support customs procedures and help move cargo through the transport chain.

  • Commercial invoice: seller, buyer, description, quantity, price and delivery terms.
  • Packing list: packages, marks, net and gross weights.
  • Certificate of origin: origin information under applicable rules.
  • Transport document: bill of lading, air waybill or road consignment note.
  • Product certificates: permits, phytosanitary or inspection documents where required.

Confirm current requirements with the exporting authority, destination importer and freight provider before dispatch.
